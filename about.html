About

Disco Nights is a record label dedicated to disco house: warm basslines, filtered samples, shimmering synths and the feel-good energy of the dancefloor.

Inspired by vintage club culture and the golden era of disco, we back artists who bring that nostalgia into the present with fresh, uplifting productions made for late nights and packed clubs.

Our first release, DN001, arrives Friday, October 9. The lights are about to go down.
